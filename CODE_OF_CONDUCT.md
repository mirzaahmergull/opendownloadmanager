# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions. Critique the work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam and sharing another person's private data are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community. Report sensitive concerns through GitHub's private reporting or moderation tools; do not post private details publicly. Decisions should be proportionate and take context into account.
